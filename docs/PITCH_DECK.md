### SLIDE 6: PREDICTION MARKET MECHANICS (POLYMARKET PROTOCOL)

* **Constant Product Market Maker (CPMM):** $k = R_{SUPPORT} \times R_{CHALLENGE}$
* **Pricing Range:** Shares trade between **$0.01 (1%)** and **$0.99 (99%)**.
* **Binary Settlement:** Winning shares redeem for exactly **$1.00 USDC**.

#### Payout Example:
* An argument starts with low consensus: priced at **$0.20 (20¢)**.
* A trader who spots newly published biomedical evidence buys 1,000 SUPPORT shares for **$200 USDC**.
* The evidence is validated; the argument resolves TRUE.
* The trader redeems **$1,000.00 USDC** $\rightarrow$ **$800 Profit (5.0x / 400% ROI)**.
